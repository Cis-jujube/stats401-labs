Daily network

Time controls

Hover, tap, or tab to a company or relationship to inspect it. All links are undirected. On small screens, scroll the charts and table sideways. Inactive companies remain visible as small nodes. New links fade in; disappearing links fade out. Playback advances one day every 900 milliseconds and stops at day 60.

How to read the network

Node color shows sector; node area increases with daily incident transaction value. Link color shows transaction type; thicker links mean larger USD amounts. Size and width scales stay fixed across all 60 days. Company positions are anchored to preserve the mental map.
