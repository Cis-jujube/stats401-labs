Lab 2: Multivariate Visualization with D3

City Multivariate Scatterplot

Population is shown on the x-axis, temperature on the y-axis, development level by circle size, and region by color. Hover over a point to see the city information.

Design Justification

Population is mapped to the x-axis and average temperature is mapped to the y-axis. I chose position for these two numeric variables because it makes the cities easy to compare. Development level is shown by circle size: Low cities use small circles, Medium cities use medium circles, and High cities use large circles. Region is shown with color because it is a category without a natural order. Each point represents one city, and hovering over a point displays the city name and all four values. I hope viewers can compare population and temperature, notice the development level of each city, and see whether cities from the same region appear in similar parts of the chart. The legends explain the region colors and development sizes.
