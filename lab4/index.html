Short Analysis

I used the 4,117 tweets in the validation split of zeroshot's Twitter Financial News Topic dataset. It contains tweet text and 20 topic categories. I checked for missing text, duplicates, and invalid topic codes; none were found. I decoded HTML entities, removed extra whitespace, and converted topic codes to readable names. All 4,117 tweets were retained.

Sentiment was calculated with cardiffnlp/twitter-roberta-base-sentiment-latest. I normalized URLs and mentions but kept punctuation, capitalization, and negation. The highest probability determines the sentiment label. The numeric score is the positive probability minus the negative probability. These are model estimates, not ground-truth labels. Each horizontal bar shows the percentages of negative, neutral, and positive tweets within one topic; the number beside it shows the sample size.

Neutral tweets make up 73.7% of the dataset. Stock Commentary has a larger positive share than Macro: 32.0% compared with 6.0%. Macro also has a higher negative share: 22.9% compared with 7.0%. These patterns describe this dataset only. Topic sizes differ, some groups are small, and the model can misunderstand financial language.
